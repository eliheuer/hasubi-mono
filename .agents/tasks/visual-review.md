# Review a draft batch

A reviewer who did not draw the batch receives the family rules, glyph names, masters and before/after proof sheets from the recorded base and candidate. Review the images independently before reading the writer's assessment.

- Compare silhouettes, counters, joins, terminals and sidebearings at the same scale, baseline and position in both states. Inspect the glyph in a line of text at terminal size.
- Check both endpoints and an intermediate weight. Include affected composites and neighboring glyphs. For Arabic, compare joining forms with the family's documented gaps. For Hebrew, check right-to-left order alongside figures and punctuation. For accents, compare the sequence and the precomposed letter.
- Give each changed glyph in each assigned master a verdict of **pass**, **revise**, or **designer decision**. Identify a fault by its location in the sheet, affected word and applicable family rule. Separate a defect already present in the base from a difference introduced by the draft.
- Return the verdicts and paths to the rendered evidence. A revised drawing needs another visual review. The designer makes the grading decision.
